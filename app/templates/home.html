How it works
List Your Asset

Create a detailed listing for your spare equipment or available land. Add photos, set your price, and define your rental terms. Our user-friendly dashboard makes it quick and easy.

Accept Bookings

Receive booking requests directly to your account. You can communicate with the renter, accept or decline the request, and manage your calendar, all from one place.

Get Paid

Once the rental period is complete, your payment is securely transferred to your bank account. Our platform handles all the financial logistics, giving you peace of mind.

Find your asset

Browse thousands of listings. Use smart search to find farm equipment or land. Filter by location, asset type, and dates.

Make a booking

Select your dates. Send a booking request. The owner will confirm the rental. For eligible listings, you can book instantly.

Start your rental

Meet the owner to get the equipment or access the land. After your rental, simply return the asset. Payment is securely released to the owner.
